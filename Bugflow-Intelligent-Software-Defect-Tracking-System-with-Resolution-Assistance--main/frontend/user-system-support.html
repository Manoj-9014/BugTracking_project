Frequently asked questions about using BugFlow.

Getting Started
What is BugFlow?

BugFlow is a bug tracking system. You use it to report problems you find, follow what happens to them, and get guidance on how to resolve them.

What can I do from my account?
  • Report Issue to submit a new bug, feature request or enhancement.
  • My Issues to see the issues you have reported.
  • Resolution Assistance to get troubleshooting guidance for one of your issues.
  • Analytics & Reports to view and download your personal report.
  • Notifications to see updates on your account and issues.
I forgot my password. What should I do?

On the login page, click Forgot password? and follow the steps to reset it.

Reporting Issues
How do I report an issue?

Click Report Issue in the sidebar, fill in the form and submit it. Enter a clear title, choose the project and category, select the issue type and severity, then describe the problem.

What information should I include?
  • A short, clear title.
  • A description of what went wrong.
  • The steps to reproduce it.
  • What you expected to happen and what actually happened.
  • Environment details and the affected modules, if you know them.

The more detail you give, the easier it is to fix.

What is the difference between Bug, Feature Request and Enhancement?

Bug is something that is broken or not working as it should. Feature Request is a new capability you would like to have. Enhancement is an improvement to something that already works.

How do I choose the severity?

Severity shows how serious the problem is: Trivial, Minor, Major or Critical. Choose Critical only for problems that block important work.

Why can't I set the priority myself?

Priority is calculated automatically by BugFlow from the severity and the category of the issue, so you do not need to enter it.

Tracking Your Issues
Where can I see the issues I reported?

Open My Issues in the sidebar to see the issues you have reported and their current status.

What do the issue statuses mean?
  • Reported: the issue has been submitted.
  • Triaged: it has been reviewed.
  • In Progress: someone is working on it.
  • Code Review: a fix is being reviewed.
  • QA Verification: the fix is being tested.
  • Resolved: the fix is complete.
  • Closed: the issue is finished.
How will I know when something changes on my issue?

Check Notifications. The bell icon at the top of the page and the sidebar badge show how many unread notifications you have.

Resolution Assistance
What is Resolution Assistance?

It gives you troubleshooting and resolution guidance for an issue you reported. Open Resolution Assistance, select one of your issues, and the guidance for it is shown.

Notifications, Reports and Account
How do I clear my notifications?

Open Notifications. Use Mark all as read to mark everything as read, or Clear notifications to remove them.

Can I download a report?

Yes. Open Analytics & Reports and use the download option to get your personal quality report as a PDF.

How do I view or update my profile?

Click your name or avatar at the top right of any page to open your profile.

How do I log out?

Click Logout at the bottom of the sidebar.
